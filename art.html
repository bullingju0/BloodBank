PAPER · INK · LANTERN

The world is cut paper. The water is living ink. The players are light.

1 · Biome Palettes

Five tones per biome (deep → bright) plus one accent that is reserved for light. Player colors never appear in the environment.

2 · Papercut Biomes

Move the mouse for parallax. Far layers fade into the water's color; near layers are dark silhouettes. The play area lives in the middle.

3 · The Divers

Four distinct silhouettes, with one big shape in the player's color. The inset shows the black silhouette test.

4 · Placeable Pieces — "Glyph Stones"

Same carved stone, different rune. The rune glows in the owner's color, so in co-op you always know who placed what.

5 · Current Toy — three ways to draw water

Left-click places a piece. Right-click removes one. Hover to see a ghost forecast of how the tide would change. Turn on Turn-based to feel the "held breath".

6 · Skill Tree as a Shared Reef

One trunk that everyone shares, with each player growing their own branch. ● unlocked · pulsing = available · faint = locked.
